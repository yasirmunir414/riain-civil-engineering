About Riain

Practical engineering. Professional delivery.

Based in County Mayo, Riain Civil Engineering supports civil engineering and infrastructure projects throughout Ireland. We work collaboratively with clients, project teams and stakeholders to deliver safe, efficient and well-managed works.

Our approach combines experienced site teams, careful planning, appropriate plant and equipment, and a commitment to completing each project to a high standard.
